# Virtual Screening and Drug Design Against Mycobacterium tuberculosis.

## Project Overview:

An in-house research project focused on "Virtual screening, drug repurposing, and structure-based drug design against Mycobacterium tuberculosis" under the guidance of Dr. Gourab Bhattacharjee at MIT ADT University.

The project involves molecular docking-based evaluation of potential therapeutic candidates and analysis of protein–ligand interactions to identify promising compounds for further investigation.

## Objectives:

* Perform virtual screening of potential therapeutic compounds
* Explore drug repurposing strategies against Mycobacterium tuberculosis
* Prepare and optimize protein and ligand structures
* Perform molecular docking studies
* Evaluate protein–ligand interactions and binding affinity
* Identify promising therapeutic candidates

## Tools & Technologies:

* Swiss-Dock
* Avogadro
* PubChem
* Open Babel
* PyMOL
* Molecular Docking
* Virtual Screening
* Structure-Based Drug Design
* Computational Drug Discovery
* Bioinformatics

## Workflow:

1. Protein structure selection and preparation.
2. Ligand identification and retrieval.
3. Ligand structure preparation and optimization.
4. Conversion and preparation of molecular structures.
5. Molecular docking using Swiss Dock.
6. Analysis of docking results.
7. Evaluation of protein–ligand interactions.
8. Identification of promising candidate compounds.
